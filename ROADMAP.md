# Chartific Standard Roadmap

**Status:** Draft (v0.1)

The **Chartific Standard Roadmap** defines the strategic evolution path for the Chartific ecosystem.

Chartific aims to establish an open standard for semantic visualization, enabling charts to become portable, interoperable, governed, authentic, and AI-native digital assets.

The roadmap describes the planned progression from foundational specifications toward a complete visualization standard ecosystem.

The roadmap is intentionally evolutionary. The objective is not only to define chart formats, but to establish the complete infrastructure required for the future of intelligent visualization.

---

# Vision

The long-term vision of Chartific is:

> To become the universal semantic layer for visualization, enabling humans and AI systems to create, exchange, govern, verify, and render charts independently of visualization engines.

The future Chartific ecosystem should allow:

```text
Human / AI Agent
        |
        v
       CQL
        |
        v
       CAM
        |
        v
       CML
        |
        v
       CAS
        |
        v
       CAR
        |
        v
 Any Visualization Engine
```

The renderer should become an implementation detail.

The semantic meaning of a chart should become the portable asset.

---

# Roadmap Philosophy

Chartific development follows several principles:

## Standards First

The specification should mature before widespread implementation.

A stable standard enables multiple independent implementations.

---

## Semantic First

Chartific focuses on the meaning of visualization rather than only the appearance of visualization.

A chart should describe:

- What information it represents.
- What relationships exist.
- What questions it answers.
- What policies apply.
- Who trusts it.

---

## AI Native

Future visualization will increasingly be generated by AI systems.

Chartific must enable AI agents to:

- Create charts.
- Understand charts.
- Validate charts.
- Govern charts.
- Explain charts.
- Trust charts.

---

## Ecosystem Driven

Chartific should support multiple:

- Renderers.
- Programming languages.
- Platforms.
- Data systems.
- AI systems.

# Success Criteria

Chartific will be considered successful when:

- Multiple independent implementations exist.
- Charts can move between platforms without rewriting.
- AI systems can generate semantic charts reliably.
- Organizations can govern chart assets.
- Users can verify chart authenticity.
- Developers can build applications without renderer lock-in.

---

# Summary

The Chartific Standard Roadmap defines the journey from a conceptual visualization standard into a complete semantic visualization ecosystem.

The objective is not simply to improve chart creation.

The objective is to redefine charts as:

- Portable semantic assets.
- Governed information objects.
- Trusted digital artifacts.
- AI-compatible visualization structures.

Through incremental development of CQL, CAM, CML, CSR, CAR, CAS, CGE, CATS, CDK, and MCP integration, Chartific aims to establish the foundation for the next generation of visualization technology.
